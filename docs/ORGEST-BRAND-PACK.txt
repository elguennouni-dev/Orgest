ORGEST — BRANDING PACK FOR SOCIAL MEDIA CONTENT
================================================
Version 1.0 · Source of truth: the product itself (frontend/src/styles.css, components/Louiz.jsx, components/icons.jsx, dict.js).
Purpose: give an AI/skill (or a designer) everything needed to produce on-brand posts, stories, reels, covers and ads.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. BRAND IN ONE PARAGRAPH
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Orgest is a SaaS for Moroccan jewelry shops and gold traders. It manages gold stock by the gram (with photos and
quantities), sales, gold exchange / old-gold buy-back, installment sales ("à tempérament / تقسيط") with exact schedules,
workshop gold tracking, printable receipts and Excel reports. Bilingual French / Arabic (RTL supported).
Instagram: https://www.instagram.com/orgest.ma/ (@orgest.ma)

2. PERSONALITY & POSITIONING
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- Core idea: "Less calculator, more sales." Orgest does the gram-exact math so the jeweler can focus on the customer.
- Feels: precise, trustworthy, warm, practical, local. A reliable counter-side assistant, not cold enterprise software.
- Is NOT: flashy luxury-for-luxury's-sake, techy jargon, crypto/finance hype, generic stock-photo corporate.
- Audience: owners and staff of jewelry shops in Morocco (often 30–60, phone-first, WhatsApp-native, speak Darija/Arabic
  and French). Secondary: artisans/workshops, gold traders.
- Proof points we can state truthfully: stock by gram with photos & quantities; sold-out items stay in history (never
  deleted); installment schedules exact to the centime; old-gold trade-in deducted on the same receipt; 80 mm and A4
  receipts; Excel reports; French + Arabic (RTL); WhatsApp reminders for due installments.
- Do not invent: user counts, testimonials, certifications, discounts, or real gold prices. Any price in an example must
  be labeled "prix fictif / مثال" (example only).

--------------------------------------------------------------------
5. TYPOGRAPHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- Primary typeface: **Readex Pro** (Google Fonts / @fontsource). Weights used: 300, 400, 500, 600, 700.
  Covers Latin AND Arabic in one family, so FR and AR posts look like one brand.
  Fallback stack: Readex Pro, system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif.
- Arabic fallback: IBM Plex Sans Arabic (already bundled in the product).
- Hierarchy for social graphics:
    Headline   Readex Pro 600–700, tight leading (1.15–1.25), sentence case. Max ~8 words.
    Sub-line   Readex Pro 400–500, 40–50% of headline size.
    Eyebrow    Readex Pro 500, UPPERCASE for Latin, letter-spacing 1.2px, in Gold Bronze (light bg) or Gold (dark bg).
    Numbers    Readex Pro 600 with tabular figures; always keep numerals left-to-right even inside Arabic text.
    CTA        Readex Pro 600.
- No script, serif or decorative fonts. No more than 2 weights per graphic. Never use all-caps for Arabic.
- Arabic text: right-to-left alignment, no letter-spacing, slightly larger size than the Latin equivalent (+8–10%),
  line-height ≥ 1.4. Mixed FR/AR in one graphic: keep each language in its own block, don't interleave words.
- Currency format: "4 656 DH" (space as thousands separator, "DH" after the number). Weight: "11,64 g" (comma decimal in
  French). In Arabic copy use "درهم" or "DH". Percent: "3 %" (French spacing).

--------------------------------------------------------------------
6. SHAPE, SPACING, SURFACES
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- Corner radius: buttons/inputs 12 px · cards 18 px · chips pill (999 px). Rounded, friendly, never sharp boxes.
- Flat design: borders (1 px #E9E2D2) instead of drop shadows. If a shadow is needed: very soft, low opacity, warm black.
- Spacing scale: 4 / 8 / 12 / 14 / 20 / 32 px; generous padding, one idea per card.
- Cards: white on Paper background, 1 px border, 18 px radius.
- Buttons: Primary = Gold fill + Ink text. Secondary = white fill + 1 px strong border + Ink text. On dark: Gold fill or
  transparent with cream text and a dark-gold border. WhatsApp CTA = WhatsApp green fill + white text.
- Dark mode composition: Ink Black background, cream/white text, gold accents, 1 px #4A3E15 borders.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
7. ICON STYLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- Style: outline (stroke) icons on a 24×24 grid, stroke width 1.8 px (2.4 px for check marks), round line caps and round
  joins, no fill, monochrome (currentColor). Simple geometric silhouettes, no gradients, no 3D, no emoji in graphics.
- Icon set used in the product (names): dashboard, sale (cart), exchange (two arrows), buy-back (arrow down to tray),
  stock (ingot/pile), workshop (hammer), calendar (installments), list, chart (bars), user, plus, search, print, warn
  (triangle), check, tag, doc, trash, menu. Add new icons in the same construction.
- Icon containers: rounded square (radius 12–14 px), 48 px, soft tint background (see Soft Category Tints) with the icon
  in the matching ink color. Feature mapping:
    Stock → gold tint · Sale → blue · Exchange → green · Buy-back → violet · Installments → rose ·
    Workshop → gold · Receipts → blue · Reports/Excel → green
- Size rules: icon ≈ 50% of its container; keep ≥ 2 px optical padding; never mix outline and filled styles.

8. MASCOT — "LOUIZ" (the gold coin)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- Louiz is a friendly golden coin character: round gold body (radial gold gradient), simple face with two dot eyes and a
  smile, short stick arms and legs in bronze (#A86F0C), soft ellipse shadow beneath. Flat vector, no outlines on the face.
- 7 moods = 7 message types (use the one that matches the content's emotion):
    wave        greeting, intro, welcome, "bonjour", launch posts
    happy       tips, positive results, default
    thinking    questions, "did you know", explaining a calculation
    worried     warnings, mistakes to avoid, late payments, risks
    proud       achievements, milestones, success stories
    celebrate   new feature, offers, mariage/Eid season, confetti
    sleepy      closing time, night/weekend, "stop doing manual work"
- Use: one Louiz per graphic, ≥ 20% of the shorter side for hero use; place on Paper, Cream, light-gold or Ink Black.
  Speech bubble: white (or gold-50) rounded rectangle, 12–18 px radius, Ink text, max 12 words.
  Louiz never holds real gold bars/cash photos; never replaces the logo; never in a different color than gold.
- Louiz's voice: warm, helpful, a little playful; talks like a good colleague at the counter ("L'astuce de Louiz : …").

9. IMAGERY & ILLUSTRATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- Preferred, in order: (1) real product screenshots (cleaned, no real customer data), (2) Louiz illustrations,
  (3) clean flat scenes of a Moroccan jewelry counter (scale, gold pieces, receipt, phone), (4) warm macro photography
  of gold jewelry on neutral warm backgrounds.
- Photo treatment: warm, soft natural light, shallow depth of field, neutral cream/black backgrounds; never harsh
  blue/cool tones; no stock handshake/laptop clichés.
- Product screenshots: put them in a rounded-corner device frame (phone or browser) with 18–24 px radius on a Paper or
  Ink Black background; crop to the one feature being talked about; blur or replace any real names/phone numbers;
  use fictional data (e.g. "Bracelet 18K — 12,00 g").
- Existing article illustrations: frontend/public/assets/blog/ (carats, inventaire, whatsapp, atelier, excel, recu,
  echange, arabe .jpg). Product screenshots: docs/screenshots/*.png.
- Do not use: real people's faces without consent, other brands' logos, real banknotes close-ups, religious imagery
  decoratively, stock photos of recognizable gold-market brands.

10. LAYOUT TEMPLATES (all graphics)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Safe margins: 6% of width on each side (stories/reels: keep 250 px clear top and 340 px clear bottom).
Sizes:
  Instagram/Facebook feed post   1080×1350 (4:5)  or 1080×1080 (1:1)
  Stories / Reels / TikTok       1080×1920 (9:16)
  LinkedIn post                  1200×1200 or 1200×627
  X / link preview (OG)          1200×630
  YouTube thumbnail              1280×720
  Carousel                       1080×1350, 5–8 slides, consistent grid
Template A — "Tip" (educational): Paper background · eyebrow (category, Gold Bronze) · headline · 1 formula block or
  example in a white card with 18 px radius · Louiz (thinking/happy) bottom corner · small logo bottom-left · handle.
Template B — "Feature": Ink Black background · gold eyebrow · white headline · phone/browser screenshot in frame ·
  icon chip + one-line benefit · Gold CTA button "Demander une démo".
Template C — "Warning / mistake": Paper background · Louiz worried · headline starting with "Erreur :" / "غلطة :" ·
  3 bullets max in a white card · Danger tint only on the small label.
Template D — "Quote/FAQ": Gold-50 background · question as headline · answer in 2–3 lines · logo.
Template E — "Carousel": slide 1 hook (big headline + Louiz) · slides 2–6 one idea each with number chip (gold circle,
  Ink digit) · last slide CTA + WhatsApp number + logo. Same background color on all slides, progress dots optional.
Formula block style (for calculations): white card, left border 4 px Gold, monospace-free Readex Pro 600 numbers,
  e.g. "Poids net = poids brut × (1 − déduction)".

11. MOTION (reels / stories / video)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- Feel: smooth and gentle, never bouncy-flashy. Ease-out curves; 250–450 ms transitions; Louiz bobs softly (1.2–3 s loops).
- Text appears by fade + 12–16 px upward slide; stagger items by 80–120 ms; numbers can count up over 600 ms.
- Max 1 transition style per video (fade/slide). No glitch, zoom-punch or shaky-cam effects.
- Structure for 15–30 s reel: hook (≤2 s, question or surprising number) → problem → Orgest solving it on screen →
  result (receipt/total) → CTA with WhatsApp number. Always add captions (FR or AR on-screen text; many watch muted).
- Sound: light, warm instrumental; no loud trending sounds that clash with a trust-based product.
- Use prefers-reduced-motion mindset: content must be understandable from the still frames.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
12. VOICE & TONE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- Tone: clear, calm, practical, respectful. Speaks to the jeweler as a professional ("vous" in French; friendly
  Moroccan Arabic/Darija in AR social, Modern Standard only for formal/legal-ish text).
- Short sentences. Concrete numbers. One idea per post. Lead with the benefit, then how.
- Preferred words FR: bijoutier, comptoir, au gramme, prix du jour, reprise d'or, à tempérament, reçu, écart de stock.
- Preferred words AR/Darija: مول الذهب، الكونطوار، بالغرام، ثمن اليوم، الذهب القديم، التقسيط، الوصل، الستوك.
- Avoid: hype ("révolutionnaire", "#1"), fear-mongering about money loss, jargon (API, SaaS, cloud) in customer-facing
  copy, guarantees we cannot keep, comparisons naming competitors.
- Emojis: sparingly in captions (max 2–3; ✨ 💛 📲 ✅ ⚖️ are on-brand); none inside graphics.
- Always label example prices: « (prix fictif, pour le calcul) » / « (مثال للتوضيح) ».
- Language rule: one language per post by default (FR or AR). Bilingual posts = two clearly separated blocks, FR first
  on LTR layouts; AR block right-aligned. Never machine-mix words.
